RUNEBOUND

— S A G A —

Recruit a company of four heroes, forge legendary rune-weapons and fight your way down five deadly dungeon floors in this original turn-based pixel RPG, made in Godot 4.

▶ Play in browser
Free · no install · keyboard, mouse or touch · progress saves automatically

Features

6 hero classes

Runeknight, Stormcaller, Thornwarden, Shadeblade, Lumen Cleric and Gearsmith — each with 4 skills and its own element.

Initiative timeline

Speed decides how often you act. See the next 8 turns and plan around Haste, Slow and Freeze.

Break & combo

Hit weaknesses to shatter enemy guards, chain hits for bonus damage and unleash a party-wide Rune Burst.

Blacksmith forge

Forge weapons from materials, upgrade them +1 to +5, socket elemental runes and salvage the rest.

Loot with affixes

Five rarities from Common to Legendary, with rolled affixes like lifesteal, crit, speed and break power.

5 dungeon floors

Procedural grid dungeons with fog of war, chests, hidden traps, healing fountains, roaming monsters and a boss on every floor.

Controls

MoveArrow keys / WASD, tap a tile, or the on-screen pad
MenusClick or tap; arrows + Enter/Space also work
TargetsTap an enemy or ally, or arrows + Enter
Pause / backEsc or P, or the II button

Tip: on phones it also works in portrait, but landscape gives the battle screen more room.
